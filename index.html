Technical detail

Product Lead · ML-powered event discovery app

A hybrid search engine over 1,901 San Francisco community events. It pairs a TF-IDF vector model with a rule-based feature-boosting layer that reads age group, cost, and time-of-day signals — reframed from a failed regression approach into a text-retrieval problem after two strategic pivots.
